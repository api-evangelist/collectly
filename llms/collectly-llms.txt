## API Reference
- [/practices/{practice_id}/appointments](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/get_practices-practice-id-appointments.md)
- [/practices/{practice_id}/appointments](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/post_practices-practice-id-appointments.md)
- [/practices/{practice_id}/appointments/{appointments}](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/get_practices-practice-id-appointments-appointments.md)
- [Update a resource.](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/patch_practices-practice-id-appointments-appointments.md): Update a resource.
- [List insurance policies](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/get_practices-practice-id-insurance-policies.md): Retrieve a list of patient insurance policies. Can be filtered by parameters such as patient_id to view specific coverage details.
- [Create an insurance policy](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/post_practices-practice-id-insurance-policies.md): Create a new insurance policy record linking a patient to an insurance company, including member ID, priority, and copay details.
- [Delete an insurance policy](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/delete_practices-practice-id-insurance-policies-insurance-policies.md): Remove an insurance policy from a patient's profile. This will archive the policy to preserve historical billing integrity.
- [List insurance policies](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/get_practices-practice-id-insurance-policies-insurance-policies.md): Retrieve a list of patient insurance policies. Can be filtered by parameters such as patient_id to view specific coverage details.
- [Update an insurance policy](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/patch_practices-practice-id-insurance-policies-insurance-policies.md): Modify an existing insurance policy. Only the fields provided in the payload will be updated (e.g., updating a priority or member ID).
- [Retrieve](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/get_practices-practice-id-patients-patient-id-subscriptions.md): Retrieve a list of patient subscriptions.
- [Create](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/post_practices-practice-id-patients-patient-id-subscriptions.md): Create a new patient subscription with PENDING status.
- [Cancel](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/delete_practices-practice-id-patients-patient-id-subscriptions-subscriptions.md): Cancel an existing subscription.
- [Retrieve](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/get_practices-practice-id-patients-patient-id-subscriptions-subscriptions.md): Retrieve a list of patient subscriptions.
- [Update](https://docs.collectly.co/reference/patch_practices-practice-id-patients-patient-id-subscriptions-subscriptions.md): Modify an existing subscription.
